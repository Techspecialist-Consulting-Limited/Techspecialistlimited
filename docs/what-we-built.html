What We Built

What happens when someone applies

The full journey, from a role opening to a hire. Each step is marked according to whether the system handles it or a person does.

System runs without anyone present Our team a person decides or acts
01

The role is set up once

Our team

HR writes the job, what a strong candidate looks like, and the topics the first interview should cover. Everything that follows runs off that one setup, so every applicant to the role is treated on the same basis.

02

A candidate applies, at any hour

System

CV and cover letter, no account to create. They receive confirmation immediately and a link they can use to check their own progress at any point afterwards.

03

The application is assessed within minutes

System

Scored against that role's criteria, with written strengths, concerns, and direct quotations from the candidate's own CV supporting the assessment. Nothing is a bare number. Whoever reads it can see the reasoning and disagree with it.

04

The shortlist forms

Our team

HR either reviews each candidate individually, or sets a standard and lets those who meet it move forward on their own. The choice is made per role, depending on volume. Nobody is ever turned down automatically.

05

The first interview happens with nobody in the room

System

A live spoken conversation with an AI interviewer, covering the topics set for the role, with time limits per topic. The candidate receives the rules by email beforehand, confirms they have understood them, and tests their microphone and camera before starting. They take it whenever suits them, including evenings and weekends.

06

The team receives the complete picture

System

A full word-for-word transcript of the interview, scores across the areas that matter for the role, and a written recommendation. The candidate arrives on the team's list marked as awaiting a decision.

07

A person makes the call

Our team

Approve, reject or hire, in one action, with the candidate notified automatically. Large groups can be handled together when volume demands it, without losing the record of what was decided.

08

The final interview is arranged

System

Virtual or in person, with the invitation and calendar entry issued automatically. Afterwards each interviewer scores the candidate separately on the same four measures, so opinions are recorded rather than negotiated in a corridor.

What this takes off the team's desk

Reading every CV that arrives in order to build a shortlist.
Writing confirmations, updates, rejections and invitations one at a time.
Finding a slot in three calendars for a first-round conversation that may last fifteen minutes.
Answering "has there been any news on my application?" by email, individually.
Chasing interviewers for the notes they meant to write up last week.
Assembling a picture of hiring activity by hand when someone asks for one.

What most employers cannot offer

These are the parts that put us ahead of standard practice rather than level with it.

Candidates are never left in the dark

Anyone who applies can check exactly where they stand, themselves, at any time, without contacting anybody. Most employers still leave applicants guessing, and it is the single most common complaint about hiring anywhere.

A real interview, on the candidate's schedule

A spoken conversation rather than a form or a recorded answer, available the moment the candidate is ready. Strong people are usually employed elsewhere and hard to reach during working hours. This removes that obstacle entirely.

Everyone is measured the same way

Same criteria, same questions, same time limits, for every applicant to a role. Not dependent on who happened to review the file, or how late in the day they got to it.

Nothing rests on memory

Every interview leaves a full transcript. Every decision is recorded with its reason and the time it was taken. If a decision is ever questioned, months later, the evidence is there rather than in somebody's recollection.

How decisions actually get made

The system assists. People decide.

It reads, scores, transcribes and prioritises. It does not hire and it does not reject. No candidate is turned down, hired or removed without a member of staff signing in and taking that action deliberately.

Candidates know before they apply.

They are told AI is used to assess applications and must agree before proceeding. The interview rules are sent to them in advance, in writing.

Everything is written down.

Every assessment carries its reasoning. Every interview leaves a transcript. Every decision is recorded with its justification and the time it was made.

Where it stands

The platform is live and running our hiring today. The capability described here is built and working, not planned.

Ongoing work is focused on strengthening the system for heavier use: resilience under high applicant volumes, tighter internal access controls, and formal rules on how long candidate information is kept. None of it changes what the platform does. It prepares it to do the same thing at greater scale and under closer scrutiny.
